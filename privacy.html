01 — Overview

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

YouTube Watch History ("the Extension") is a browser extension developed and maintained by minimum effort ("we", "us", "our"), operating at minimumeffort.ca. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

By installing or using the Extension, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please uninstall the Extension immediately.

02 — Data Access

What Data the Extension Accesses

The Extension reads your browser's local history solely to identify YouTube videos you have previously watched. Specifically, it accesses:

  • URLs from your browsing history that match the pattern youtube.com/watch
  • Page titles associated with those URLs (video titles)
  • Visit timestamps and visit counts as recorded by your browser
  • Videos you manually add to Favorites, which are stored in local extension storage

All data access happens entirely within your browser on your device. No data is ever transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any external server, database, or third party — with the sole exception of license key validation described in Section 5.

03 — Data We Do Not Collect

We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

  • Your Google or YouTube account credentials or account data
  • Your full browsing history beyond YouTube watch URLs
  • Your IP address or geographic location
  • Device identifiers, hardware information, or operating system data
  • Cookies, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting data of any kind
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or behavioral data
  • Any data from websites other than youtube.com
04 — Local Storage

How Data Is Stored

The Extension stores the following data locally on your device using built-in local storage.

  • Your Favorites list (videos you have starred within the Extension)
  • Your Pro license key and activation status, if you have upgraded
  • Your preferred sort order setting

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any cloud service, not accessible to us, and not shared with any third party. You can clear this data at any time by removing the Extension.

Important: If you clear your browser history, all YouTube watch history displayed in the Extension will be permanently lost, as the Extension reads directly from browser history. Favorited videos stored in extension storage will not be affected by clearing browser history, but will be lost if you uninstall the Extension.

05 — License Validation

Pro License Key Validation

If you purchase a Pro license, the Extension sends your license key to a secure validation server operated by us via Cloudflare Workers for the sole purpose of verifying the validity of your license. During this process:

  • Only your license key string is transmitted — no personal data, no browsing data
  • The validation request is processed by Creem.io, our payment and licensing provider
  • No record of your identity, device, or usage is stored by us as a result of this request
  • After successful validation, your activation status is stored locally on your device only

For Creem.io's own privacy practices, please refer to their privacy policy at creem.io.

06 — Third Parties

Third-Party Services

The Extension displays video thumbnails loaded directly from YouTube's public image servers (img.youtube.com). This is a standard browser image request and does not transmit any personal information beyond what your browser normally sends with any web request (e.g. your IP address to YouTube's servers). We have no control over YouTube's data practices. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how YouTube handles such requests.

We do not integrate any advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporting tools, or social media trackers into the Extension.

07 — Children's Privacy

Children Under 13

The Extension is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since the Extension does not collect personal information from any user, there is no special risk to children's privacy beyond what is described in this policy.
