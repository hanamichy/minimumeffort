ⓘ Important note

YouTube Watch History reads directly from your browser's local history — it does not store, collect, or transmit any of your data to external servers. If you clear your browser history, all watch history will be lost and cannot be recovered. Videos you have added to your Favorites are stored locally in your browser's extension storage and will persist unless you manually remove them or uninstall the extension.
